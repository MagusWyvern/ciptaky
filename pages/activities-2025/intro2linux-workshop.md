# Intro2Linux Workshop

Event date: 21-22nd June 2025

The "Introduction to Linux" workshop is a two-day, hands-on event designed specifically for KYUEM students to explore the fundamentals of the Linux operating system. 

In an increasingly technology-driven world, proficiency in Linux is a valuable asset for students across various disciplines, including computer science, engineering, data science, and beyond, opening doors to numerous career opportunities and advanced studies.

Many students may have limited or no prior exposure to command-line interfaces or the Linux environment, which can often seem intimidating at first.

This workshop aims to explore Linux by providing a beginner-friendly, interactive, and supportive learning environment, facilitated by experienced users and committee members.

Over the two sessions (Saturday and Sunday, 2-4 hours each day), participants will transition from basic concepts to practical skills, empowering them to confidently navigate and utilize a Linux system.

The workshop will emphasize hands-on practice over passive listening, to make sure participants actively engage with the material and build foundational competence.
