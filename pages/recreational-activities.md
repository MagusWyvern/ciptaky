# Recreational Activities

Recreational activities are things people do for enjoyment, relaxation, or personal fulfillment outside of work and obligations. They help recharge the mind and body.

# Past events

1. [Pongal Festival Celebration](./activities-2025/pongal-festival-celebration.md)

Pongal is a traditional South Indian harvest festival, predominantly celebrated by the Tamil community, and observed annually in mid-January. It marks the end of the harvest season and serves as an expression of gratitude for agricultural abundance.
